Why it matters

A bad lesson stays.

When your AI only reads something, a hidden instruction is a one-time problem. When your AI learns from it, that instruction can become part of how it thinks, and you can no longer search for it.

The more personal your AI gets, the more it matters what gets in. That is why RA(I) sits at the door, not inside the model. Signal, not verdict: we show you what we see, you decide. We know nothing about you, and the checks run where your AI runs.
